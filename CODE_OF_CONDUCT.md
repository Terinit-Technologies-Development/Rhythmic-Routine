# Contributor Code of Conduct

## 1. Our Standard

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for everyone contributing to or discussing **Rhythmic-Routine**.

We expect all contributors, issue reporters, and community participants to:
- Practice respectful, collaborative communication.
- Engage in constructive technical disagreement focused on code, architectural principles, and user privacy.
- Treat fellow community members with dignity, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

---

## 2. Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are strictly unacceptable:
- Harassment, derogatory comments, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting, or disruptive behavior.
- Disclosure of personal, confidential, or private data (doxxing), including pasting personal chat logs, tokens, or private identifiers.
- Introducing malicious code, intentional security backdoors, or unauthorized data collection into the codebase.

---

## 3. Enforcement

Project maintainers from **Terinit Technologies** are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

Maintainers reserve the right to edit, close, or remove issues, comments, or pull requests that violate this Code of Conduct, and to temporarily or permanently suspend offending accounts.
