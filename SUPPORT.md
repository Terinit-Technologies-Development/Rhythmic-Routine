- **Service-Level Agreement (SLA):** Support for personal use is provided entirely on a voluntary, best-effort community basis. There is no guaranteed response time or resolution SLA.
- **Self-Hosting & Manual Builds:** Refer to [`PLATFORM_SETUP.md`](PLATFORM_SETUP.md) and [`docs/qa/pass-04d-owner-v1-test.md`](docs/qa/pass-04d-owner-v1-test.md) for self-compilation instructions.
