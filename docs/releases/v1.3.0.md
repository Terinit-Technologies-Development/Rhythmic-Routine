## Release summary

Routine remains the ecosystem's policy and native enforcement authority. This
release introduces the Attention-Day restorative model and coordinates with
Reader's outward-attention evidence and Meditation's inward/restorative evidence.

- Attention Day allocation with Morning Meditation requirement and retained
  state across the scheduled morning boundary.
- Restorative Gates for CD4+ with a provider choice bound to the exact gate.
- CD3 Reader Daily Baseline: 3600 verified seconds and 36 qualified pages.
- CD4+ provider choice: Reader Recovery Session V1 at 1800 seconds plus 11
  qualified pages, or Meditation at 1800 qualified seconds.
- Meditation substitutions capped at two per Attention Day.
- Cross-app Attention Insights and Evening Meditation signals through
  signature-protected, narrow local IPC.
- Native restorative enforcement and policy projection diagnostics, including
  provider-bound recovery and cooldown-preserving reconciliation.
- HyperOS Accessibility detection repair and legacy migration compatibility.

The frozen re-entry invariant is `cooldown elapsed AND required restorative
condition satisfied`. Restorative activity never shortens a cooldown. Existing
`LEGACY_READING` obligations retain their migrated requirements (for example,
5400 seconds / 47 pages); only newly allocated CD4+ Restorative Gates use the
discrete 1800 / 11 Reader model.

This discrete gate model explicitly supersedes the v1.2.0 release-note wording
that each later cooldown added 30 minutes and 11 pages. CD4+ uses the fixed,
per-gate provider choice above; it does not accumulate hidden Reader debt.
