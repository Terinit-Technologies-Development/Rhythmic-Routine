## 3. Preserved Android Signing Relationship

Routine and Reader communicate through the `RECOVERY` permission:

- Permission: `com.terinit.rhythmicreader.permission.RECOVERY`
- Protection level: `android:protectionLevel="signature"`

This architecture is unchanged. The accepted pair works because both builds
share a compatible signing identity. For future internal releases:

- Routine and Reader must continue to use the same stable signing identity.
- Do not remove signature protection or downgrade the protection level.
- Do not change the permission name, package names, or provider authorities.
- Do not introduce a new signing key during this release line without shipping
  both applications with the new identity.
