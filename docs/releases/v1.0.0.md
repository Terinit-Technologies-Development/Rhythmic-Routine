# Rhythmic-Routine v1.0.0 Release Notes

**Release Date:** September 1, 2026  
**Classification:** Experimental V1 (Source-Available)

---

## 1. What V1 Is

Rhythmic-Routine v1.0.0 represents the first feature-complete baseline of our local-first digital wellbeing application. It translates the principles of natural daily cadence into functional on-device mobile software.

Instead of arbitrary daily countdown timers that leave you locked out when you most need your phone, Rhythm creates structured focus boundaries, groups related infinite-scroll applications together, provides restorative "Touch Grass" breaks, and respects essential communication utilities.

---

## 2. Why Rhythm Exexists

Modern mobile operating systems treat screen-time limits as numerical punishments: after 60 minutes on an app, you are abruptly blocked until midnight. Users quickly learn to tap "Ignore Limit for 15 Minutes" or disable limits entirely because real life is dynamic.

Rhythm approaches attention mindfully:
- **Intention Over Punishment:** Boundaries exist to protect morning focus and evening sleep.
- **Grouped Session Accounting:** 10 minutes on X plus 15 minutes on Instagram represents 25 minutes of continuous feed consumption. Rhythm accounts for switching within a group.
- **Restorative Cooldowns:** Reaching your session limit invites you into a gentle, offline recovery period with grounding prompts rather than a lockout.
- **Inviolable Essential Apps:** Phone, Maps, Camera, and Clock are never blocked, ensuring device safety and peace of mind.

---

## 3. Core Behavior & Features

1. **Morning Buffer:** Keep designated social and entertainment feeds paused until your morning start time.
2. **Evening Wind-Down:** Pre-sleep boundary that pauses addictive feeds and encourages disconnecting.
3. **Risk Groups:** Monitor related scroll-heavy applications collectively with configurable thresholds and recovery cooldowns.
4. **Touch Grass Overlay:** Fullscreen, opaque (`#FAF7F0`) calm intervention screen that gracefully navigates back to Android Home.
5. **Temporary Access Leases:** Need urgent access? Grant a temporary emergency override without losing track of your underlying routine or cooldown state.
6. **Local SQLite Insights:** On-device weekly rollups and rolling consistency scores without external tracking.

## 4. Platform Qualification

### Android: Physically Hardware Validated
- Verified on physical Android hardware across Pass 04C and Pass 04D.
- Standalone QA APK (`app-qaStandalone.apk`) installs and runs completely offline without Metro or PC tethering.
- Targeted launcher `<queries>` (`ACTION_MAIN` + `CATEGORY_LAUNCHER`) discover installed apps without requiring `QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES`.
- `RhythmEnforcementService` (Accessibility) observes window changes and intervenes immediately when a restricted package is foregrounded.

### iOS: Experimental Source Implementation
- Source architecture implemented using Expo Config Plugins, Screen Time `ManagedSettingsStore`, and `DeviceActivity` extensions.
- **Not physically tested or qualified on Apple hardware.** Real-device verification remains pending Apple Family Controls distribution entitlement assignment and macOS build validation.

---

## 5. Local-First Privacy Architecture

Rhythmic-Routine is designed with zero cloud tracking:
- **No User Account:** No signup, email, phone number, or login required.
- **No Cloud Backend:** No remote servers, databases, or cloud sync endpoints.
- **No Analytics or Ad SDKs:** Zero telemetry, tracking pixels, or third-party monitoring libraries.
- **Data Stays on Device:** Application lists, usage timestamps, and routine settings are stored exclusively in local SQLite.
- **Minimal Native Observation:** Accessibility service inspects foreground package identity only (`canRetrieveWindowContent="false"`). Screen text, messages, passwords, and form inputs are never read.

---

## 6. Known Limitations

- **Experimental Software:** This software is an experimental digital-wellbeing tool provided on an "AS IS" basis.
- **Android OEM Aggression:** Aggressive background task killers on certain Android OEM distributions (MIUI, ColorOS, EMUI) may terminate background accessibility services unless battery optimization exemptions are granted.
- **User Agency & Circumvention:** Rhythmic-Routine is not designed as anti-tamper or security software. Users with administrative access can toggle off Accessibility permissions or force-stop the application.
- **iOS Not Validated:** The iOS implementation has not been tested on physical hardware and should not be relied upon for active enforcement.
- **No Cross-Device Sync:** Data is confined to the individual physical device; uninstalling the app removes local SQLite databases.
- **No Store Binaries:** Official public distribution through Google Play and the App Store is not yet live.
